Lists and data

List rows for things people open by their title, tables for records compared by column, facts for the properties of one thing. Nothing is ever cut without saying so.

{%- call cat::entry("rows", "List rows", "Stable") %}
Purpose
Things people open by their title: issues, merge requests, repositories, notifications, commits, changes. Rows separated by lines, not cards: a state icon, the title, labels, and one line of secondary facts; counts at the end.
Markup
ul.item-list > li.item-row with span.item-state, div.item-main (a.item-title, span.item-labels, p.item-meta), and span.item-count.
Variants
Issue, merge request (with its checks), notification (unread rows keep the title's weight), repository, commit, change, label, milestone (with progress.milestone-progress), a change of a merge request's stack (its title leads to the change's page; its change ID, its commit, flags, checks as a status line, a note, and the form that picks its change), the versions of a change (ol.version-list: the shown version marked with aria-current and not a link, its facts, checks, and how it was matched; then form.version-picker, two labeled selects that compare any two versions). Rows that can be changed end with div.item-actions, icon buttons with tooltips. Compact density removes padding, never text size.
Accessibility
A list; the state icon has its word for screen readers; the title is the row's one link.
Not for
Records compared by column (a table); the properties of one thing (facts).

{%- endcall %} {%- endcall %} {%- call cat::entry("table", "Table of records", "New") %}
Purpose
Records compared by column: tokens, keys, sessions, members, users, deliveries. Below 40 rem of page width each row becomes a block: the first cell as its title, the others as labelled lines, the row's actions at its end, so no value or action is off screen.
Markup
div.table-wrap[role=region][tabindex=0][aria-labelledby] > table.table.table-records with a caption; every td carries data-label with its column's name; the actions cell is td.table-actions.
Variants
Wide (a table); narrow (stacked blocks). Empty: an empty state instead of the table.
Accessibility
A real table with column headers and a caption; stacked, the column names show as labels and stay in the header for screen readers.
Not for
Things opened by title (list rows); layout.

{%- endcall %} {%- endcall %} {%- call cat::entry("pager", "Pager", "New") %}
Purpose
More results: "Next page", and "Previous page" where the list has it, as buttons in a navigation region below the list. A list that loads in place keeps the rest of the page.
Markup
l::pager(prev, next); an empty link leaves that button out, and with neither the pager isn't shown.
Variants
Next only; previous and next; previous only.
Accessibility
A nav named "Pages"; links with rel=prev and rel=next.
Not for
Steps of a process.
{%- call cat::example("Both directions.") %} {%- call l::pager("#pager", "#pager") %}{% endcall %} {%- endcall %} {%- endcall %} {%- call cat::entry("more", "And N more", "New") %}
Purpose
A short list in a limited space (a details column, a card, a requirement) shows up to five items, then "and N more", which links to the full list. N is at least 2: with six items, all six show, since "and 1 more" would take the place of the item it hides.
Markup
The first five items, then l::more(href, n); six or fewer items without it.
Variants
None.
Accessibility
A link that says how many more there are.
Not for
Lists with their own page (they use the pager).
