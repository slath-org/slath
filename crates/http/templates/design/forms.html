Forms

A form is fields, questions, and groups of fields, then one row of buttons. Everything is required unless its label says "(optional)". After a failed submit the error summary leads the form, each field shows its message, and what was entered stays.

{%- call cat::entry("form", "Form", "New") %}
Purpose
Holds a form's parts at the items distance, at the width of single-line fields (32 rem); the wide form (48 rem) holds long text and the Markdown editor. Controls fill the form's width.
Markup
form.form-layout or form.form-layout-wide, with fields, questions, groups, then f::buttons. A form that isn't the page's only one sits in a section or a frame with a title.
Variants
Normal; wide. States: as entered; failed (error summary first, see Messages).
Accessibility
Every control has a visible label; errors are linked from the summary and named by aria-describedby.
Not for
A single button that acts at once (an inline action); filters of a list (the filter disclosure).

    Purpose
    One value: its label, the control, an optional hint (what to enter and what follows from it), and its error, 4 px apart.
    Markup
    f::field(id, label, hint, error, optional) around the control; the control carries f::described(id, hint, error). Labels are short nouns without a colon; hints are sentences.
    Variants
    Required; optional. States: default, focus, invalid (error under the hint, stronger border), disabled, read-only.
    Accessibility
    The label names the control; hint and error describe it; invalid controls are aria-invalid.
    Not for
    A choice among options (a question); a value to copy (copy value).

    {%- endcall %} {%- endcall %} {%- call cat::entry("question", "Question and option", "New") %}
    Purpose
    A question with options to choose: a legend in the label's style, then checkboxes or radios. Each option's name is in regular weight, with its explanation under the name.
    Markup
    f::question(id, legend, hint, error) around f::option(kind, id, name, value, label, checked, hint) calls.
    Variants
    Radios (one answer); checkboxes (several). With or without explanations. States per option: default, focus, checked, disabled; the question: invalid.
    Accessibility
    A fieldset whose legend names the group; each option's label names its control and is a full-size target.
    Not for
    Alternatives that show different content (the choice component); a single on/off setting (one option without a question, a checkbox).

    {%- endcall %} {%- endcall %} {%- call cat::entry("option-follow-up", "Option with a follow-up", "New") %}
    Purpose
    An option of a question whose fields apply only while it's chosen, such as whom an approval leaves the decision to. They sit under the option's name, marked by a line at their start, and are hidden while another option is chosen.
    Markup
    f::option_follow_up(kind, id, name, value, label, checked, hint) around f::field calls, among the question's f::option calls.
    Variants
    Chosen (fields shown) or not (hidden); fields with errors. Without :has(), the fields always show, under their option.
    Accessibility
    The fields follow their option in reading and tab order and have their own labels; hidden fields are out of the tab order. Choosing the option needs no script.
    Not for
    Alternatives that each show their own content (the choice component); fields that apply to every option (put them after the question).

    Purpose
    Two or more fields that belong together, under a legend in the subsection heading's style; its fields keep the items distance, and the group is the groups distance from what's around it.
    Markup
    f::field_group(legend) around the fields, inside a form.
    Variants
    None.
    Accessibility
    A fieldset named by its legend.
    Not for
    One question (a question); a whole form (its section heading names it).

    {%- endcall %} {%- endcall %} {%- call cat::entry("form-buttons", "Form buttons", "New") %}
    Purpose
    The form's buttons, one row at its end: the primary action first, at most one primary, each at its own width. In a details column they fill the column.
    Markup
    f::buttons() around the buttons, last in the form. Labels are verbs with their object: "Create token", "Save rule".
    Variants
    One or several buttons; a quiet "Cancel" last.
    Accessibility
    Real buttons; reading order is the visual order.
    Not for
    Actions on the whole page (the page heading's actions).
    {%- call cat::example("Primary, secondary, cancel.") %} {%- call f::buttons() %}{% endcall %} {%- endcall %} {%- endcall %} {%- call cat::entry("copy-value", "Value to copy", "New") %}
    Purpose
    A value to take elsewhere: a clone URL, checkout commands, a new token. Read-only, monospace, selected as one piece, with a copy button.
    Markup
    f::copy_value(ctx, id, label, value, copy_label). A new secret shows once, in the success summary, with "It won't be shown again."
    Variants
    On its own; in a menu (Clone, Check out), in div.menu-panel.copy-panel, copy-panel-wide for long commands. Without scripts the copy button stays hidden and the value can be selected.
    Accessibility
    The field is labelled; the button is named ("Copy the token") and reports the copy in a toast.
    Not for
    Values to edit (a field).

    Purpose
    Destructive actions, at the end of the page or of the part they act on: a heading, what happens, and a danger button that names what goes. Irreversible ones ask the person to type the name.
    Markup
    f::danger(id, title, text, level) around the form; level 2 at the end of a page, 3 inside a part of it.
    Variants
    One button; with a typed confirmation.
    Accessibility
    A section named by its heading. The red frame only adds to the words.
    Not for
    Reversible actions (they happen at once, with the way back next to them); removing one's own things (one named button).
