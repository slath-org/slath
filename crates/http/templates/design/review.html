Timeline and status

The same fact always looks the same: a status is an icon and words in one of three tones, a decision page answers "can this move forward?" first, and the history is one timeline, oldest first.

{%- call cat::entry("status-line", "Status line", "Stable") %}
Purpose
A status in one line: an icon, the words, and a reason. Three tones only: done (check), waiting (clock: normal, nothing wrong yet), and blocking (cross: something must change). Waiting is never red, so red keeps meaning "act". Used for requirements, checks (also in the details column), and deliveries.
Markup
A line of its own: m::status_line(ctx, tone, text), a p.status-line.tone-{done|waiting|blocking} (icon, a small gap, then the words). In a list: li.tone-* with m::tone_icon(ctx, tone), then the words in a span; checks in a details column are a ul.plain-list of these.
Variants
The three tones; with a reporter and a time on a second line.
Accessibility
The icon is decorative; screen readers hear the tone as a word ("Done:", "Waiting:", "Blocking:") before the reason. Color only adds to icon and words.
Not for
An issue's or merge request's state (state label); categories (badge). A state that blocks, such as Conflict or Unresolved, is a status line, never a red badge.

{%- endcall %} {%- endcall %} {%- call cat::entry("readiness", "Readiness card and requirement rows", "Stable") %}
Purpose
At the top of a decision page and again where the person acts: a headline, a reason, one primary action chosen for the viewer, and the requirements as status lines. A requirement that isn't done opens in place to its fix and its evidence.
Markup
section.readiness.tone-* with .readiness-head (icon, h2.readiness-title, reason, action) and ul.requirements of li.requirement; an open one is a details whose summary ends with the turning chevron.
Variants
The three tones; with or without an action (no action in the merge box's copy).
Accessibility
A section named by its headline (the card headline, 18 px, keeps its h2 level). No progress bars: they rely on color and hide which item failed.
Not for
Pages where nothing is decided.

{%- endcall %} {%- endcall %} {%- call cat::entry("timeline", "Timeline", "Stable") %}
Purpose
The one history of an issue or merge request, oldest first: comments with a header line and their text, events as single lines with an icon and the actor first, consecutive events folded into one line, reviews with their verdict, and the marker of what's new since the viewer's review. Filters may narrow it but never split or reorder it.
Markup
ol.timeline of li.timeline-comment (an article with header.comment-header, the text, and div.comment-actions for the author and moderators), li.timeline-event, li.timeline-event.timeline-group (a disclosure), li.timeline-review, and li.since-marker. Names are account names; times are the time component.
Variants
Comment (edited, hidden, deleted); event; event group; review (approved, changes requested, stale); since marker.
Accessibility
An ordered list; each comment is an article named by its author; icons are decorative.
Not for
Lists of things (list rows).

{%- endcall %} {%- endcall %} {%- call cat::entry("thread", "Review thread", "New") %}
Purpose
A conversation on a line, a file, or the whole merge request: where it is and on which revision, the code it's about, its comments (a suggestion under its comment), and its end: the reply box and, for those who may, "Resolve thread". One markup in the conversation and in the diff.
Markup
li.timeline-comment.timeline-thread > article with the header, the code snippet, ol.thread-comments, and div.thread-actions holding d::reply_box and the reply-resolve button.
Variants
Current; from an earlier revision; outdated; resolved; addressed by a bot; with a finding (a bot's, in a neutral category).
Accessibility
An article named by its place; the code is a scrolling region; bot text carries the evidence chip and the Bot badge.
Not for
Comments on the whole issue (timeline comments).

{%- endcall %} {%- endcall %} {%- call cat::entry("stack", "Stack and change steps", "New") %}
Purpose
On a change's page, the merge request's stack in the details column, bottom first, with each change's position, subject, short ID, and status lines (landed, new or returned in this revision, unresolved, conflict, verdicts, checks); the shown change is marked. Above the change, one group per kind of step, [‹ Change 2 of 6 ›] and [‹ Version 1 of 3 ›], with arrow buttons to the previous and next one. Only for a stack of more than one change.
Markup
section.details-section > ol.plain-list.stack-list > li.stack-item ([aria-current=true] for the shown change) with span.stack-position and div.stack-main (a link, or strong for the shown change, then status lines, m::status_line). The steps: nav.cluster.change-steps of div.step-group[role=group], each an icon link (a.button.button-quiet.button-icon[data-key] with aria-keyshortcuts and its name in words), span.step-label naming the group, and the other icon link.
Variants
With removed changes: a second section, "Removed changes", each with the revision that removed it. At an end, its arrow stays in place, dimmed and hidden from screen readers (.step-none), so the label doesn't move. The version group only appears for a change with more than one version.
Accessibility
An ordered list, so its order is announced; the shown change is named "(shown)" for screen readers. j and k (changes), [ and ] (versions) act only while focus is inside the change view and do exactly what the buttons do; each button's tooltip names its key.
Not for
Navigation between pages of a context (side menu); a commit list (item list).

{%- endcall %} {%- endcall %} {%- call cat::entry("chips", "Badges, state labels, counts, labels", "Stable") %}
Purpose
Small facts beside text, each for its own job: a badge for a category or flag (Private, Archived, Bot, Outdated); the state label for an issue's or merge request's state; a count for a number; a label as a color dot and its name; the evidence chip for what kind of evidence something is (observed, reported, claimed).
Markup
span.badge; span.state-label.state-{open|closed|merged|draft|iterating} with its icon; m::tab's or span.count; a.label-chip with its swatch; m::evidence(kind).
Variants
As listed. No filled, saturated badges.
Accessibility
Words, never color alone; a state label's icon is decorative.
Not for
Statuses of checks or requirements, and states that block such as Conflict (status line).
