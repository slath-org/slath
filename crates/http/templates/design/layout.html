Layout

Space shows what belongs together. These components place their children at one of four distances, inside 4 px, items 16 px, groups 24 px, and sections 32 px; compact density makes each one step smaller (switch it in the Appearance menu). Components have no outer margin of their own, so these are the only source of space between them.

{%- call cat::entry("stack", "Stack", "New") %}
Purpose
Puts its children one under the other at one distance: stack-inside for the parts of one element, stack for items of a group, stack-groups for groups in a section, stack-sections for sections.
Markup
<div class="stack"> around the children; any element can be the stack.
Variants
The four distances. No states.
Accessibility
Layout only: no role, no name. The reading order is the source order.
Not for
Controls side by side (use a cluster); a group that distance alone can't set apart (use a frame).

{%- endcall %} {%- endcall %} {%- call cat::entry("cluster", "Cluster", "New") %}
Purpose
Controls side by side in one row, 8 px apart, wrapping on narrow screens: buttons of a form, chips, a field and its button.
Markup
<div class="cluster"> around the controls.
Variants
None. No states.
Accessibility
Layout only. The controls keep their own names; their order is the source order, also when the row wraps.
Not for
Text with controls in it (use running text); a form's buttons (use form actions, which is a cluster with its rules).

{%- endcall %} {%- endcall %} {%- call cat::entry("page", "Page and page heading", "New") %}
Purpose
The page: its heading, then its sections, 32 px apart (24 px in compact). The heading holds the one h1, an optional state line under it, the page's actions on the title's row, and an optional one-sentence introduction.
Markup
div.page > header.page-head (div.page-head-row with the h1 and div.page-head-actions; optional p.page-head-meta and p.page-head-intro), then the sections.
Variants
With or without actions, state line, introduction. On narrow screens the actions wrap below the title.
Accessibility
Exactly one h1. The actions follow the title in reading order. At most one primary action.
Not for
The code pages, whose h1 is for screen readers and whose place shows in the breadcrumb.

{%- endcall %} {%- endcall %} {%- call cat::entry("section", "Section", "New") %}
Purpose
A part of the page with an h2: the heading 12 px above its content, the content's items 16 px apart, and the page's stack 32 px above the next heading. Actions that apply to the whole section sit at the end of the heading's row.
Markup
section.section[aria-labelledby] > h2 (or div.section-head with the h2 and its actions), then the content.
Variants
With or without heading actions.
Accessibility
The section is named by its heading. Levels are never skipped.
Not for
A part of a section (use a subsection); a group that needs a border (use a frame).

{%- endcall %} {%- endcall %} {%- call cat::entry("frame", "Frame", "New") %}
Purpose
A group that distance alone can't set apart, such as a step of the merge box or a form among other forms: a border, the frame's padding (16 px, 12 px in compact), and an h3 title 8 px above its content.
Markup
section.frame[aria-labelledby] > h3.frame-title, then the content. Tones: frame-notice, frame-warning, frame-danger.
Variants
Plain; notice (information); warning; danger (the danger section). In a stack-groups, frames are 24 px apart.
Accessibility
Named by its title. The tone is never the only sign: the title and text say what it is. Forced colors show the border.
Not for
Every group (most need only distance); nesting frames in frames.
