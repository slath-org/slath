Frame

Each level of navigation has one form: the global bar, the side menu, the context header, tabs, and view menus. Nothing deeper gets navigation of its own.

{%- call cat::entry("global-bar", "Global bar", "New") %}
Purpose
At the top of every page: the side menu toggle, the product, search, and the person's own places (review requests, notifications, profile, settings, administration, appearance, sign out). One row at every width: below 40 rem the person's own places move into the account menu behind the avatar.
Markup
base.html; nothing else draws it. Its icon-only links and buttons have their names for screen readers and in tooltips.
Variants
Signed out (search, appearance, sign in); signed in, wide; signed in, narrow (account menu); administrator (one more place).
Accessibility
A navigation region named "Main"; the account menu's button is named "Account" with the person's name; Escape closes it.
Not for
Places of a context (the side menu).

This page's own bar is the example: narrow the window below 40 rem to see the account menu.

{%- endcall %} {%- call cat::entry("side-menu", "Side menu, tabs, view menus", "New") %}
Purpose
The side menu names the context and lists its areas; tabs are the sections inside an area; a view menu shows views of one list. Every page with a context has the side menu, in the same form; it collapses on wide screens and opens as a drawer on narrow ones.
Markup
m::side_menu and m::side_item; m::tabs and m::tab; m::view_menu and m::view_item.
Variants
Repository, organization, user settings, administration, the person's work; this catalog.
Accessibility
Navigation regions named by their context; the current area, section, or view is marked as current.
Not for
Actions (menus and buttons).

Purpose
Where you are, at the top of every page of a context, and the actions on the whole context: a repository (owner / name, visibility, Watch, Fork), an organization, a user, a bot. User settings and administration show their context in the side menu only.
Markup
Repositories: repo/header.html. Others: fr::context_header(ctx, kind, name) around the actions.
Variants
Repository; organization; user; bot. With or without actions; on narrow screens the actions wrap below the name.
Accessibility
The name links to the context's front page; the kind is a word.
Not for
The page's title (the page heading follows it).
