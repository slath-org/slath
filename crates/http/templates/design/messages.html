Messages and states

What happened decides the kind of message. A toast disappears, so it never carries something the person must act on.

Choosing a message

Which message follows what
What happenedMessage
A form was submitted and the page reloadedSuccess summary at the top of that form's section
A form failedError summary leading the form, and each field's own message
The page or its subject is in a state the person should knowNotice at the top of the page, information or warning
One part of the page needs a wordNotice line in that part's frame
A part loaded in place failed, or an action without a page load finishedToast
{%- call cat::entry("notice", "Notice", "New") %}
Purpose
A state of the page or its subject the person should know: the repository is archived, being imported, read-only for maintenance; or a warning about something that may need action, such as a failing webhook.
Markup
msg::notice(tone) with one or more paragraphs; tone is info (default) or warning. With the one action that resolves it on the same row: msg::notice_action() with a paragraph and the button. At the top of the page, under its heading.
Variants
Information; warning; with an action (the action wraps below the text on narrow screens).
Accessibility
role=note: read in place, not announced. The tone is in the words too; color only adds to them.
Not for
The result of a form (success or error summary); a part of the page (notice line); errors.

{%- call msg::notice() %}

This repository is archived. It's read-only.

{% endcall %} {%- call msg::notice("warning") %}

Purpose
What a submitted form did, in the past tense with its object: "Webhook saved."
Markup
msg::saved(text), first in the section of the form that was sent. A new secret shown once adds its value to copy and "It won't be shown again."
Variants
Text only; with a value to copy.
Accessibility
role=status: announced politely after the page loads.
Not for
Results without a page load (toast); anything the person must still do (notice).
{%- call cat::example("After saving.") %} {%- call msg::saved("Webhook saved.") %}{% endcall %} {%- endcall %} {%- endcall %} {%- call cat::entry("error-summary", "Error summary", "New") %}
Purpose
Leads a form that failed: the heading "There is a problem", then one link per invalid field that says what to enter. Each field also shows its own message, and everything entered stays.
Markup
msg::error_summary() with one <li><a href="#field"> per field, first in the failed form, not at the top of the page.
Variants
One or several errors. An error of the whole form (a conflict) is an item without a link.
Accessibility
role=alert, receives focus after the submit; each link moves focus to its field. The heading is a message heading (15 px).
Not for
Failures without a form (toast or notice); a field's own message (under the field).

    Purpose
    A region being replaced (a page loading, a list changing its view) keeps its content and shows a thin bar along its top until the new content arrives.
    Markup
    Any region with aria-busy="true"; the scripts set and clear it around every in-place load.
    Variants
    Moving bar; still under reduced motion. A submitting button shows its spinner instead.
    Accessibility
    aria-busy tells screen readers to wait for the new content; focus stays or moves to the new region's heading.
    Not for
    Work that takes longer than a page load (a fork, an import): those show a notice and a state.
