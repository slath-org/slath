Disclosure and menus

Everything that expands or folds is a disclosure or a menu. Its control says what opens and ends with a chevron that turns when open, and the same control closes it again. The reply box is the one exception.

{%- call cat::entry("disclosure", "Disclosure", "New") %}
Purpose
Content that most people don't need at first, opened in place: filters, evidence behind a summary, the built-in paths of a setting, help for a step.
Markup
d::disclosure(ctx, summary, open) around what opens. The summary names what opens ("Filters", "Built-in paths (20)").
Variants
Closed; open (the chevron turned). Opened by default where the content is active ("Filters (active)").
Accessibility
A native details: the summary is a button that reports expanded or collapsed; Enter and Space toggle it; nothing traps focus.
Not for
Actions (a menu); alternatives (the choice component); anything needed to finish the task.

Purpose
Actions on the page's subject ("More actions", "Check out") and views of a list ("State: Open"). Its button names what it holds or which view is shown and ends with a chevron that turns while it's open.
Markup
d::menu(ctx, label, icon, start) around the panel's items: d::menu_link, buttons with menu-item, headings. icon makes an icon-only button that keeps label for screen readers; start opens the panel towards the end, for buttons at a row's start.
Variants
Labelled; icon-only (still with the chevron). Items: link, button, current view.
Accessibility
Escape closes it and returns focus to its button; a click outside closes it; the panel stays inside the screen.
Not for
Navigation between areas (side menu, tabs); a single action (a button).

Purpose
The end of a review thread: a one-line "Reply…" box that looks like the place to write and grows into the reply form, with the cursor in it. Cancel folds it back. While it's open, resolving the thread is a checkbox in the form instead of a separate button, so nothing typed is lost; with "Add to my review", the thread is resolved when the review is submitted.
Markup
d::reply_box() around the form; its buttons end with d::cancel(). In a thread's actions, the separate "Resolve thread" button (reply-resolve) sits beside the box and hides while it's open.
Variants
Closed; open; open with a started reply (the browser restored it: it opens again).
Accessibility
The summary is a button named "Reply…"; opening moves focus into the text; Cancel returns focus to it. Without scripts it still opens; only Cancel needs the script.
Not for
Comments on the whole merge request or issue (the comment form at the end of the page).
