Basic controls

A link goes somewhere; a button does something. Tooltips name icon-only controls and show a value's full form; they work with a mouse, the keyboard, and touch, and never hold something only they say.

{%- call cat::entry("links", "Links and buttons", "New") %}
Purpose
A link goes to another page or a place on this one; a button changes data, opens a menu, or copies. A link's text names where it goes, never "here" or "more". A link to another site ends with the external-link icon.
Markup
<a href> for links, c::external(ctx, href, text) for other sites; <button> for actions.
Variants
Link; link to another site; button (see the buttons in the overview).
Accessibility
A link never submits and a button never only navigates, so each behaves as its role says. Links to other sites say so to screen readers.
Not for
Actions that look like links (they're quiet buttons).

{%- endcall %} {%- endcall %} {%- call cat::entry("tooltip", "Tooltip", "New") %}
Purpose
Names an icon-only control, or shows a value's full form: the exact time of a relative one, a full hash, a cut text. It appears on hover, on keyboard focus, and on a tap; Escape, a tap elsewhere, or scrolling closes it.
Markup
data-tip="text" on the element; c::icon_button(ctx, icon, name) for icon-only buttons. The browser's title isn't used: touch screens and keyboards never show it.
Variants
Below the element, or above it where there's no room; always inside the screen.
Accessibility
Never the only place of something needed to act: an icon-only button has its name for screen readers too. Where the text adds to the element's name, screen readers get it as the element's description.
Not for
Help or anything longer than a phrase (a popover or a hint).

{%- endcall %} {%- endcall %} {%- call cat::entry("time", "Time", "New") %}
Purpose
A moment: relative within seven days ("3 hours ago", "in 2 days", "yesterday"), a date otherwise ("14 Nov 2026"), and the exact date and time with its zone in the tooltip. Where the exact moment is the point (the audit log, deliveries, sessions, tokens, administration) it's shown in full.
Markup
c::time(t), or c::time(t, true) for the full form; t is the page's time value.
Variants
Past; future; older than a week; full.
Accessibility
A time element with its exact value; the short form is the text read aloud.
Not for
Calendar dates without a time (a milestone's due date).
{%- call cat::example("Recent, upcoming, older, and in full.") %}

  • last used {% call c::time(recent, true) %}{% endcall %}
{%- endcall %} {%- endcall %} {%- call cat::entry("hash", "Hash and path", "New") %}
Purpose
A commit hash shows its first 12 characters, linked, with the full hash in its tooltip. A file path wraps after a slash, never inside a name.
Markup
c::hash(oid, href); c::path(path).
Variants
None.
Accessibility
Both stay left to right in every language; the full hash is the link's description.
Not for
Change IDs (shown in full, 8 characters).

{%- endcall %} {%- endcall %} {%- call cat::entry("cut", "Cut text", "New") %}
Purpose
Text cut short where its space is fixed (a table cell, a list row, a menu item): it ends with "…", and its full form is in the tooltip and on the page it links to. Titles, names, and branch names wrap instead.
Markup
c::cut(text) inside the fixed space.
Variants
None.
Accessibility
The whole text stays in the page for screen readers; the tooltip shows it on hover, focus, and tap.
Not for
Anything a person must read in full to act.
