People

Wherever Slath writes an account's name, its avatar comes right before it, for every name in a line. Text written by people (Markdown, mentions) shows names as written, and Git identities, which anyone can write into a commit, get a neutral person icon.

{%- call cat::entry("account", "Account name", "New") %}
Purpose
A person's, organization's, or bot's name in status lines, timeline events, headers, list facts, details columns, check reporters, menus, and tables: the avatar, then the name, then the Bot badge for a bot.
Markup
p::account(ctx, name): one link to the account, also in a list row's facts. p::account(ctx, name, false) only where the name sits inside another link, which can't hold a second one.
Variants
Linked; inside another link or a summary (plain text); a bot (with its badge); uploaded or generated avatar. An account that no longer exists is p::deleted(ctx, text). Where a page holds who did something, p::who(ctx, who, start, link) writes any of them, and Slath itself (the merge queue, jobs) or someone unknown (a push no account made) as words.
Accessibility
The avatar is decorative; the name is text inside the same link, so there's one target and one tab stop.
Not for
Names in Markdown; Git authors and committers (Git identity); the global bar's own avatar and profile pages (larger avatars).

{%- endcall %} {%- endcall %} {%- call cat::entry("git-identity", "Git identity", "New") %}
Purpose
The author or committer written into a commit. When the commit's email address is a confirmed address of an account (any of its addresses, or its no-reply address), it's that account: the account name with its avatar, and the name as written in the commit in the tooltip, so one person's commits under several addresses all show as their one account. A commit a bot pushed is the bot's: the bot is its committer, and its author unless a person's confirmed address is written there. An address no account has confirmed is shown as written, with a neutral person icon. Who pushed, merged, or applied a suggestion is an account Slath authenticated, said in words.
Markup
p::git_identity(ctx, name, account): account is the matched account's name, or empty.
Variants
Matched by address (an account name); pushed by a bot (the bot); unmatched (the person icon and the name as written).
Accessibility
The avatar and the icon are decorative; a matched name links to the account.
Not for
Accounts Slath knows from a session or token (the account name); matching by name or by an unconfirmed address.

Several accounts in a small space, such as the reviewers in a list row: up to three avatars side by side, then "+N".
Markup

Variants

Not for
