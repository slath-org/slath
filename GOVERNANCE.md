# Governance

This document describes how decisions about Slath are made and how that is
meant to change as the project grows.

## Roles

**Contributors** are everyone who takes part: by reporting issues, reviewing,
writing code or documentation, testing, or helping other users.

**Maintainers** review and merge pull requests, triage issues, handle
security reports and Code of Conduct reports, and make releases.

The **lead maintainer** decides where maintainers can't agree and is
responsible for the project's direction until the model below changes.

## How decisions are made

Most decisions happen in issues and pull requests. A maintainer approves and
merges a pull request when it fits the project's direction and meets the
requirements in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

Larger changes, such as a new feature, a new dependency, a change to the data
model or to a public interface, or a change of policy, start as an issue. The
change goes ahead once a maintainer agrees with the approach.

While the project has fewer than three maintainers, the lead maintainer makes
the final decision.

Once there are three or more maintainers, decisions are made by **lazy
consensus**: a proposal is accepted if no maintainer objects within seven
days. If maintainers object and can't resolve it in discussion, the
maintainers vote and a simple majority decides. The lead maintainer breaks
ties. Changes to this document, to the license policy, or to the Code of
Conduct need a two-thirds majority of all maintainers.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors who have made sustained, high-quality contributions over several
months, review the work of others constructively, and follow the Code of
Conduct can be invited to become maintainers. Any maintainer can propose
someone. The current maintainers decide as described above, and the new
maintainer must accept.

Maintainers who are inactive for six months, or who ask to step down, become
emeritus maintainers. They can return by asking the other maintainers.
A maintainer can be removed for serious or repeated violations of the Code of
Conduct.

## Licensing

Slath stays free software. Contributions are accepted under the license of
the files they change and the Developer Certificate of Origin, with no
contributor license agreement (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
Relicensing AGPL-licensed parts therefore needs the consent of every
contributor whose work is affected.

## Code of Conduct

Everyone taking part in the project follows the
[Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). The maintainers enforce it.
